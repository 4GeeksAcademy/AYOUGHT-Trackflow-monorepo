# Project Brief

## 1. Company Overview

Company name: HealthCore

Industry: Outpatient healthcare services

Business description:
HealthCore operates 12 clinics across the United States and the United Kingdom, providing primary care, specialist consultations, chronic disease management, and preventive health programmes. Its approximately 200 employees support clinical care and business operations across both countries.

## 2. Business Problem

HealthCore's growth has outpaced its technology and operating infrastructure. Its clinics rely on disconnected EHR, billing, and scheduling systems, with separate processes across locations and countries. This fragmentation contributes to a 22% appointment no-show rate, a 14% US claims denial rate, manual compliance and workforce tracking, clinical documentation overhead, and delayed, inconsistent management reporting. It also makes it difficult to provide staff with complete patient histories across locations while meeting HIPAA and UK GDPR obligations.

## 3. Project Objectives

Build the systems, workflows, and intelligent tools needed to operate HealthCore as a safe, efficient, patient-centred modern healthcare provider. The intended outcomes include connected access to patient and operational data; improved booking, reminders, and follow-up; reduced clinical and billing administration; stronger compliance monitoring and auditability; better workforce processes; reliable operational telemetry; and timely, unified reporting for department leaders and executives.

## 4. Target Users

Patients booking and receiving care; clinical staff across 12 clinics; patient experience and access teams; revenue cycle and billing staff; compliance and data governance staff; HR and workforce teams; the technology team; department managers; and executive leadership.

## 5. Business Requirements

Required capabilities described in the briefing:

- Provide unified online appointment booking, reminders, proactive outreach for appointments at risk of no-show, and patient experience reporting.
- Unify access to patient, appointment, billing, and staff data across existing systems, including patient history across locations.
- Assist clinical documentation and claims review, suggest billing codes from clinical notes, analyze denial patterns, and support denied-claim follow-up.
- Provide unified billing and executive dashboards with current cross-location and cross-country measures, plus scheduled reports and threshold alerts.
- Consolidate data access auditing, support patient data requests, and monitor compliance risks under HIPAA and UK GDPR.
- Support HR requests and onboarding, credential checklists, CME tracking and expiry alerts, workforce KPIs, and common employee queries.
- Provide technology telemetry, centralized monitoring and logging, automated health checks and alerts, data pipelines, and searchable technical documentation.

## 6. Business Constraints

HealthCore handles protected patient data and must account for HIPAA in the United States and UK GDPR in the United Kingdom. Systems must respect the distinct requirements of both jurisdictions. The solution must work across 12 locations in two countries and integrate with a heterogeneous legacy estate, including two EHR platforms and separate billing and scheduling processes. Patient privacy and complete auditability are essential; the briefing does not specify a project budget or delivery dates.

## 7. Project Roadmap

The briefing identifies the expected deliverables by business area: unified patient and operational data services; booking, reminders, and no-show outreach; clinical documentation and claims assistance; compliance monitoring and patient data request automation; HR and CME workflows; technology monitoring and data pipelines; and clinical, departmental, and executive dashboards and reports. It does not define implementation phases, sequencing, or dates; these require approval and planning by HealthCore Digital.
