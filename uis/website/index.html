About HealthCore

Founded in 2011 in Austin, Texas, HealthCore is an outpatient healthcare organization dedicated to making quality medical care accessible and convenient.

Founded by Dr. Sandra Okonkwo, HealthCore has grown into a network of 12 clinics serving communities across Texas, Florida, Georgia, London, and Manchester. Our approximately 200 employees support clinical care, operations, and patient services.
